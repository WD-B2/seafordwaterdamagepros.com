The first 24 to 72 hours

What happens in the first 24 to 72 hours after water damage

Water damage restoration is a race against moisture. Within the first day, water wicks up drywall, soaks carpet padding and finds its way under baseboards. By the second and third day, wet organic materials start to support mold growth, which is why drying begins as early as the structure allows.

That is why this site spends so much time on the first hour. The steps below describe the usual order of a job. Timing varies with the water source, the size of the loss and the materials involved, so treat it as a map, not a promise.

Stop the sourceShut off the main valve, the appliance supply or the fixture. Cut power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and safe to reach.Every extra gallon adds drying time and cost.
ExtractionPumps and extraction units remove standing water. Wet carpet pad and soaked insulation may be pulled.Removing liquid water is faster than evaporating it.

Safe to do while you wait

Stop the source if you can reach it safely. Lift valuables and electronics off wet floors. Keep people and pets out of standing water, especially if it may be sewage or has touched outlets. If water reaches a panel, outlet or gas appliance, call your utility or 911 before the restoration line.

Emergency situations

When water damage is an emergency, and what to do first

Treat it as an emergency when water is still flowing, when it is rising, when it has reached electrical equipment, or when it came from a drain or toilet. Those are the cases where hours change the outcome. A slow stain on a ceiling can usually wait for a morning call. A burst supply line cannot.

The emergency steps are the same for most sources. Find the main water shutoff and turn it clockwise. Photograph the damage before you move anything. Then call the 24/7 line so the extraction and drying plan can start while you handle the insurer. A pipe that has burst is covered on the burst pipe page, and a rising flood has its own section on the flood damage page.

Water is flowing

Shut the main or the fixture valve. Open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line if the plumber says it is safe.

Water touched power

Stay out. Cut power only from a dry panel you can reach safely. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk first.

It came from a drain

Treat it as contaminated. Keep out, keep pets and children out and read the sewage backup guide.

Storm or flood water

Floodwater is rarely clean. Do not wade through it. See storm water damage for what to document.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can a crew get to my home in Seaford?

The dispatch line is open 24/7. No arrival time is promised, because it depends on crews on duty and conditions. Call and you will hear a straight answer for your address.

What should I do before anyone arrives?

Stop the source if you can do it safely, usually the main water shutoff. Photograph the damage, lift valuables off wet floors and stay out of water that may be contaminated or has touched electrical equipment. Call your utility or 911 for gas or electrical risk.

Will my homeowners insurance cover it?

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, and gradual leaks and neglect often are not. New York's Department of Financial Services notes that a typical homeowners policy does not cover flood. Read your policy and ask your insurer, since only they can say.

How long does drying take?

Many small losses dry in about three to five days, and larger or wetter ones take longer. A crew confirms dryness with moisture readings, not by touch. Hardwood, plaster and insulation usually need more time than carpet and drywall.

Do I need a waterproofing contractor instead?

Restoration repairs damage that already happened. Waterproofing changes the building so future water stays out. Seepage through a foundation is a waterproofing question, and a failed sump pump is a plumbing question, so ask us about both.

Do you handle mold too?

Yes, as a secondary service after the moisture is controlled. In New York, a mold project over 10 square feet generally needs a licensed assessor and a separate licensed remediator. See the mold remediation page for how that works.

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Seaford?

Yes. The line is staffed 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. If water is flowing, shut the main and call. If it touched power or came from a drain, stay out and follow the safety steps above first.
