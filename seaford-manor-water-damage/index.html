Safety checklist before you call

  • Keep people and pets away from water that may be contaminated or touching electrics.
  • Do not operate breakers or plug in equipment while standing in water.
  • Shut off the water supply only if it is safe and accessible.
  • Photograph visible damage before anything is moved or removed.
  • Protect unaffected belongings only where it is safe to reach them.
  • Call 911 or your utility for gas odor, sparking or any immediate danger.

Clean water from a supply line is usually the easier case, but it still soaks drywall, insulation and flooring quickly. Sewage and drain backups are different, and household fans are not a safe way to dry them.

Extraction, drying and insurance documentation

Standing water is removed first, then the structure is dried using moisture readings, air movers and dehumidifiers. The water extraction page explains the first stage, and the structural drying page explains the rest. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for this work, and you can ask any company how it applies it. Not every wet material can be saved.

For an insurance claim, photos, a record of when you found the leak and moisture readings can help describe the loss. Coverage depends on the cause and your policy, so ask your insurer before assuming anything is covered. Nothing here promises coverage or approval.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Seaford Manor?

The dispatch line is answered 24/7, including nights and weekends. No arrival time is promised, and availability is confirmed on the call.

My water heater is leaking. What should I do first?

Close the cold water inlet valve if it is safe and reachable, keep out of any water touching electrics, and call. If a gas heater smells of gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911.

How do I know if a leak is inside the wall?

Stains, bubbling paint, a musty smell and a spinning water meter with every fixture off are all clues. Moisture readings confirm it without opening everything.

Is sewage cleanup different from clean water cleanup?

Yes. Sewage is contaminated water, so affected materials are assessed and handled differently from a clean supply-line leak. Do not try to dry it with household fans.

Will my insurance pay for the cleanup?

That depends on the cause of the water and on your policy. Call your insurer, ask what your policy covers, and keep photos and notes from the start.
