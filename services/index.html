Water damage looks similar on the surface and behaves very differently underneath. A supply line that burst, a leaking water heater and a flooded canal each call for different first steps. The groups below follow the source of the water.

Not sure which fits? Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. Dispatch will point you to the right service and explain scope and price before work starts. Return to the home page for the overview of the whole process.

Water jobs on these pages are guided by ANSI/IICRC S500, and mold jobs by ANSI/IICRC S520. Both are published industry standards, and you can ask any company how its crews apply them.
