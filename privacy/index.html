Privacy Policy

A short, plain description of what information this site and phone line handle.

Who we are

Seaford Water Damage Pros operates seafordwaterdamagepros.com and a 24/7 phone line for water damage restoration and mold remediation in Seaford, NY. Our address is 4034 Sunrise Hwy, Seaford, NY 11783. Contact: contact@seafordwaterdamagepros.com.

Calls

When you call, we collect the details you share, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the damage, so the job can be dispatched. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

This website

This site has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. Like most sites, our hosting provider may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited to keep the site secure and working. Embedded Google Maps may set cookies under Google's own policies.

How information is used

Information from calls is used to dispatch and perform the work you request, to answer questions about it and to improve service. We share it only with the restoration professionals handling your job and where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@seafordwaterdamagepros.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
